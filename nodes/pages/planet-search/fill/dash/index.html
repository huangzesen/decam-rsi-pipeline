How this works and how to stop it
  • The gap list: every usable public DECam exposure (g r i z Y VR, 30 s or longer) with a full InstCal set at NOIRLab, minus what NERSC already holds (the DESI Legacy Surveys mirror and our first and second 5 % downloads).
  • Downloads run at NERSC only: four jobs in the free transfer queue on login nodes and two regular CPU nodes charged to $NERSC_CPU_ACCOUNT. Vista nodes are not used, since every Vista node-hour costs SUs.
  • Every 30 minutes a relay on the Mac sends newly finished files to Vista in one Globus copy and deletes their NERSC copies once it succeeds. The third 5 %'s gap files stay at NERSC for its search.
  • Old images (first and second 5 %) move to Vista in copies of about 0.5 TB; each is deleted at NERSC as soon as its copy succeeds, so space frees step by step.
  • Vista scratch has no quota but deletes files nobody has read for 10 days; searches run at NERSC and copy back what they need.
  • Stop all downloads: create $NERSC_SCRATCH/decam-fill/STOP_ALL. Stop the relay: planet-search/fill/relay/STOP on the Mac.
  • This page replaces the "DECam 5% Search" and "DECam Slice Atlas" pages. Claude updates it from NERSC, Vista and Globus status; it does not refresh by itself.
