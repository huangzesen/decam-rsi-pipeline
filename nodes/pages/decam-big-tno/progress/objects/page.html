The catalogue

The database. Every body here has a folder, rsi/decam-big-tno/candidates/C###/, with its card (every identity: catalogue number, labels, each search list it appeared in, region, rank, scenario and detections), the text of every verdict, its history, and copies of the evidence files (lead check, novelty check, extended arc, fixed-source test, other-archive check, MPC tracklet matches, follow-up batch). Catalogue numbers are permanent; new bodies from later searches get the next number, and a body that later fails keeps its folder, marked dropped. Rebuilt by candidates/refresh.sh.

Sizes come from our own H (good to about ±0.3 mag), for albedo 0.10; a darker surface (0.05) makes a body 1.4 times larger. Short arcs give loose orbits: the few bodies with arcs of days that fall outside a chart are drawn as arrows at its edge.

This page carries no sky positions and no observation dates of unreported bodies; the folders do.
